Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “Patterns of Participation in a Student-Led Reading Club,” as a Research Article in the International Journal of Participatory Learning Spaces.

This study describes how speaking turns were distributed during 20 observed reading club sessions. We recorded speaking turns and examined the distribution of participation. Across the sessions, the three participants with the most speaking turns in each session accounted for an average of 46% of that session’s turns. These observations provide descriptive evidence to inform the design of participation opportunities in reading clubs. The study did not measure reading proficiency or learning outcomes and therefore does not establish effects on either.
