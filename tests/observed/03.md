Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript entitled “A Descriptive Study of Library Study-Space Preferences” as a Research Article in the Journal of Learning Environments in Practice.

This study examines which study-space attributes students prioritize. Using descriptive statistics from a questionnaire completed by 64 students, we found that 42 respondents identified quietness as the most important attribute. The study describes preferences within this sample and provides a basis for further research on study-space design. It does not assess learning outcomes or establish whether these preferences extend to a broader student population.

The manuscript aligns with the journal’s focus on empirical research concerning learning spaces, user experiences, and learning environment design. Its account of students’ stated preferences may inform questions for future research into how study spaces can respond to users’ priorities.
