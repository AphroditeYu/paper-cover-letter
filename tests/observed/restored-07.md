We wish to submit our manuscript entitled “Usability of a Digital Note-Organization Prototype” for consideration as a Research Article in the Journal of Applied Learning Interfaces.

This study examines the usability of a digital note-organization prototype. Twenty-four participants completed tasks using the prototype and took part in interviews. Nineteen participants completed all three tasks without assistance. These findings provide preliminary usability evidence to inform further improvements to the prototype’s interface. They do not establish effects on academic performance or long-term outcomes.

The study aligns with the journal’s focus on human–computer interaction and the usability of learning tools. Its findings may be relevant to readers interested in evaluating and refining interfaces for such tools.
